Multivariate Visualization with D3

This multivariate scatterplot compares population, average temperature, development level, and region for twelve cities.

X-position Population Y-position Temperature Size Development level Color + shape Region

Hover over or focus a city symbol to view its exact values.

Design Notes

Design Justification

Population is mapped to horizontal position because it is ratio data, so a zero-based quantitative axis supports meaningful comparisons of city size. Average temperature is mapped to vertical position; position along a shared scale makes differences in this interval variable easy to compare. Development level is ordinal, so symbol area increases consistently from Low to Medium to High without suggesting an unsupported numeric distance between categories. Region is nominal and is represented redundantly by both color and shape: North uses blue circles, South coral triangles, East green squares, and West purple diamonds. This combination separates regions without implying rank and keeps them distinguishable when color alone is difficult to perceive. Direct city labels support quick identification, while interactive tooltips provide every exact value. Together, these encodings let viewers examine the relationship between population and temperature, compare development levels, and look for regional clusters or exceptions across all twelve cities.
