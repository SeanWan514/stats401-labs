Design Description

Why These Encodings Work

Both treemaps map GDP to rectangle area because area directly communicates each country's contribution to the total. A shared categorical color scale maps Increase, Unchanged, and Decrease to coral pink, light pink, and deep plum, allowing status comparisons without changing the quantitative area encoding. Continent and area outlines preserve the World–continent–area–country hierarchy, while labels and tooltips identify individual countries and provide exact GDP values. The squarified layout creates relatively compact rectangles, making country areas and labels easier to compare across the display. In contrast, slice-and-dice alternates horizontal and vertical subdivision by depth. Its elongated bands make parent–child nesting clearer, but smaller countries can become narrow and harder to compare. Showing both methods with identical data and encodings isolates the effect of segmentation strategy: Squarify supports value comparison, whereas SliceDice emphasizes hierarchical structure.
