STATS 401 · Lab 4

Cleaning Web Data for Visualization

A reproducible Python pipeline cleans 1,500 tweets, estimates sentiment with RoBERTa, and prepares tidy data for D3.

Dataset Davidson et al. hate-speech and offensive-language corpus SourceMIT-licensed GitHub repository Records analyzed 1,500 tweets Sentiment model CardiffNLP Twitter RoBERTa

Method and Limitation

The source provides raw tweet text and human annotations for hate speech, offensive language, or neither. Those annotations are used only as the comparison attribute. Sentiment is recalculated from every tweet with cardiffnlp/twitter-roberta-base-sentiment-latest. The probabilities and labels are model-generated estimates, not ground truth; sarcasm, slang, reclaimed language, and limited context can produce errors.

D3 Visualization

RoBERTa Sentiment by Annotation Category

Each bar totals 100%. Move your pointer over a segment, or focus it with the keyboard, to inspect its count, share, and average sentiment score.

Sentiment score equals P(positive) minus P(negative), ranging approximately from −1 to 1.
